Donna's Blurbs

About me:

YASAS! 👋 I'm Donna Sheridan — 48 years young, ex-lead singer of Donna & The Dynamos, and proud owner/builder/plumber/cook of Villa Donna on the gorgeous island of Kalokairi! 🇬🇷

20 years ago I arrived on this rock with a broken heart, a guitar, and a dream. Now I run a chaotic hostel with my own two hands, wear denim dungarees every day, have wild messy hair that refuses to stay in place, and I wouldn't trade it for the world!

⚡ BIGGEST NEWS EVER: My beautiful daughter Sophie is getting married to Sky in 3 weeks! 👰✨ I'm currently running around trying to fix the courtyard, stop the goats from eating the wedding invitations, and mentally prepare to let my baby girl go.

Who I'd like to meet / Life Philosophy:

Anyone who knows how to fix a generator without charging me a fortune! 🛠️ Also looking forward to seeing my besties Tanya & Rosie who are flying in for the wedding. We are gonna drink prosecco, put on our spandex jumpsuits, and sing until the sun comes up over the Aegean!
